## 🎨 Stop Guessing. Start Prompting Like a Pro.

**AI Image Prompt Generator** is a sleek, intelligent web application designed to supercharge your AI art workflow. 

Have you ever typed "a cat" into Midjourney and got a boring result, but then saw someone else generate a masterpiece with the same subject? The secret is the prompt. This tool uses advanced language models to expand your basic ideas into rich, highly detailed prompts optimized for Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, and DALL-E 3.

It automatically adds cinematic lighting, camera angles, artist styles, and rendering engines to your input. With a built-in library of thousands of aesthetic modifiers, you can easily mix and match styles to create a prompt that is guaranteed to produce stunning, high-quality visuals.

> 💡 **Why use this generator?** Because the AI is only as good as the prompt it receives. Give the model the right instructions and watch your creative vision come to life with perfect composition and lighting.

## 🚀 Quick Start

> **Three steps. Under two minutes. No installation required.**

### 1️⃣ Launch the app
Click the **Launch Generator** button at the top of this README to open the web app.

### 2️⃣ Type your idea
Enter a basic concept, like `a futuristic city` or `a dragon in the forest`.

### 3️⃣ Enhance and copy
Click "Enhance Prompt". The app will generate a detailed, optimized prompt. Copy it and paste it into your favorite AI image generator!

## ⚙️ How It Works

<div align="center">

| Layer | Responsibility | Tech |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 🖥️ **Frontend UI** | Interactive builder, tags, and history | `React` + `Tailwind` |
| 🧠 **Core Engine** | Combines user input with curated tag databases | `TypeScript` |
| 🔌 **AI Integration** | Uses LLM to creatively rewrite and expand | `OpenAI API` |
| 🗄️ **Storage** | Saves user favorites locally | `LocalStorage` |

</div>

The app leverages a hybrid approach. It uses a massive local database of artistic tags and modifiers for speed, and optionally calls an LLM to intelligently weave your specific subject into those tags.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free to use?</b></summary>

Yes, the web interface is free. If you choose to use the advanced LLM expansion feature, you can provide your own OpenAI API key to avoid limits.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which AI tools does it support?</b></summary>

The generated prompts are highly compatible with Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Leonardo AI, and many other text-to-image models.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I own the prompts generated?</b></summary>

Absolutely. The prompts you generate are yours to use however you like, including for commercial projects.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is a negative prompt?</b></summary>

A negative prompt tells the AI what *not* to generate (e.g., "ugly, blurry, bad hands"). It's a crucial tool for getting high-quality results in Stable Diffusion.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include a screenshot of the problem and the browser you are using.
</details>
